How it's measured

  • A collector records the raw feeds (bbo, l2Book, trades, activeAssetCtx). analytics/report.py replays them every hour.
  • Maker and taker come from users[] plus the aggressor side, checked against userFills.crossed. Duplicate trades after a reconnect are dropped by tid.
  • The mid comes from bbo. If it goes quiet for over 60s the collector was down, and any markout touching that window is dropped.
  • Depth comes from the 20-level book. Bands it doesn't reach show as ≥ floors.
  • Maker P&L leaves out fees, rebates and inventory. It measures adverse selection, not profit.
